# Agent Note: Hero hover keeps three breathing lights

Status: implemented

## Problem

The hero hover painted a blurred gradient around the whole card. The face and the letters sat inside one halo.

## Decision

Hover keeps the thin border glow and adds three soft blocks: one large and two small, sized by the golden ratio, set toward the edges. They grow from under half size in 1.2s. That first brightness is the peak. Each light then breathes on its own loop by receding and returning, never brighter than the appear. The three loops stay out of phase. Text stays above the blocks.

## Alternatives considered

- Border glow only. Rejected: the block lights are the hero hover; the border is only the edge.
- Three equal blobs. Rejected: the large one has to read as the primary light.

## Consequences

The full-card halo is still gone. The three blocks do not share one breath, so the card does not pulse as a single disc. Light mode paints the blocks and the 8px rim darker and more saturated so they read on a white card. Dark mode keeps the paler lights.
